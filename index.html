Research

Intelligence as search.

When a robot cannot accomplish a task, does it need a different strategy, a better representation, or a new physical capability? My research connects this question to autonomous systems operating in the real world.

01 / Embodiment

Expanding what robots can physically do

I develop heterogeneous robotic systems that combine aerial access with ground mobility and manipulation. The UAV–miniUGV system and MiniUGV₂ enable exploration and inspection in confined spaces. My ongoing work asks how an agent can recognize limitations of its current embodiment and reason about useful changes.

Heterogeneous systems · Confined-space inspection · Embodiment search

UAV–miniUGV research ↗
02 / Representation

Making the world useful for action

My work on visual navigation studies how robots can make decisions directly in image space. Pareto-optimal visual decision-making and traversability prediction connect perception to navigation while accounting for sensing and computational constraints.

Visual navigation · Traversability · Image-space decisions

Selected visual navigation publications ↓
03 / Policy

Choosing actions with bounded resources

I investigate bounded-rational decision-making and coordination through informed prior policies and context-generative default policies. Autonomous racing provides a demanding setting for connecting localization, planning, and control to physical performance and reliability.

Bounded rationality · Multi-robot coordination · Autonomous racing
