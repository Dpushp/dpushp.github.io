Full-scale autonomous racing

Connecting localization, perception, planning, and control under demanding physical constraints.

Paper ↗Watch video ↗

Overview

I led the IU–Luddy Autonomous Racing Team from 2023 to 2026, coordinating technical development, integration, mentoring, and track testing. Our work brought together localization, perception, planning, and control on a full-scale racing platform. At Purdue, I contribute to research and development with Purdue AI Racing, including mentoring LiDAR-based localization research.

Highlights

  • Leadership of autonomy-stack development and multidisciplinary system integration at IU.
  • IU–Luddy team results including second place at the 2025 LVMS time trial with a 164 mph average and fourth place at Laguna Seca.
  • Research on modular racing stacks, localization, and visual autonomy.
