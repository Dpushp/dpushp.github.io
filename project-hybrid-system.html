UAV–miniUGV hybrid exploration

Aerial access with ground exploration and manipulation.

Paper ↗Watch video ↗

Overview

The UAV–miniUGV system combines complementary physical capabilities to reach and inspect spaces that neither robot can fully address alone. A tethered miniature ground robot extends the UAV’s operational reach into confined areas and supports object search and manipulation.

Highlights

  • A heterogeneous aerial–ground platform with a deployable miniature robot.
  • An autonomy pipeline for confined-space search, manipulation, and return.
  • Physical experiments demonstrating the combined system.
