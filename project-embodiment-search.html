Recognizing when a robot’s physical capabilities are insufficient for its task.

Watch video ↗

Overview

A failed task may reflect inadequate sensing, mobility, actuation, energy, representation, or strategy. My ongoing research asks how a robot can distinguish these causes and reason about changes to its embodiment within limits on information, computation, and intervention cost. This is a research direction, rather than a completed autonomous redesign system.

Research questions

  • Task-dependent diagnosis of physical capability limitations.
  • Coupled reasoning over embodiment, representation, and policy.
  • Resource-aware evaluation of candidate physical adaptations.
