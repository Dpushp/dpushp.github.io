Aerial–ground robots for hidden spaces

Complementary robots that extend access, mobility, and manipulation.

Paper ↗Code ↗Watch video ↗

Overview

Some missions require both aerial access and ground operation. MiniUGV₂ expands the capabilities of a UAV–ground robot team through a compact tracked platform, articulated arms, integrated sensing, and an attachable magnetic tether interface. The work studies platform design and experimental operation for hidden-space inspection.

Highlights

  • A compact UAV-deployable ground robot with articulated arms and integrated sensing.
  • A tether module and electro-permanent magnetic head supporting deployment and optional detachment.
  • Physical experiments on mobility, self-righting, and manipulation.
