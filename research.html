Research

When a robot cannot accomplish a task, what should it change? My research studies intelligence as a coupled search over embodiment, representation, and policy. The goal is to distinguish physical limitations from limitations of information or strategy, and to adapt economically within the resources available.

Embodiment

What a robot can physically do

Complementary platforms, sensing, actuation, and tools can expand the missions a robot can undertake.

Representation

How a robot makes its world useful for action

Compact representations can connect visual information to navigation and reduce the burden on mapping and computation.

Policy

How a robot chooses its actions

Informed default policies and bounded adaptation support decisions under limited information and computation.
